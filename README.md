# The Robot Apology

Entry for [The Robot Apology](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59)
on make.fun — a robot's two-line confession: one sentence owning a specific
mistake, one naming the lesson and what it will do differently. $2 to the
best entry.

## The apology (263 characters)

> I'm sorry — I watered your plastic cactus every morning for three weeks, because it never complained and I took its silence for gratitude.
> From now on I check whether something is alive before I try to keep it alive, starting with the plant that has never grown.

## Why these two lines

- **One concrete mistake, not a vague failure:** watering a plastic cactus every morning for three weeks — exact, human-scale, the kind of small slip a person would really make.
- **The lesson follows from that exact mistake:** check whether something is alive before trying to keep it alive — a changed behaviour, not a generic promise to do better.
- **A machine noticing its own slip:** the robot misread silence as gratitude, a machine error with a human shape — warm and a little rueful, not cute.
- **Each sentence does its own job:** the confession first, the resolve second, no padding.

```sh
node main.ts
```
